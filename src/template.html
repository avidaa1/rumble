Controls

Keys WASDMove A D, drop through S, jump W or Space. J light, K heavy, L special, U dodge, I shield. Hold W or S with special for up or down specials.
Keys arrowsArrows move and jump. Comma light, Period heavy, Slash special, Semicolon dodge, Quote shield.
PS4 padStick or D-pad move. Cross jump, Square light, Triangle heavy, Circle special, L1 or L2 dodge, R1 or R2 shield. Options pauses. Menus: D-pad or stick, Cross select, Circle back, Square cycles back.
TouchOn-screen pad: arrows, Jump, Hit, Heavy, Spec, Dodge, Shield. Up plus Spec is the recovery move.
TipsHigher percent flies further. Double jump, then your up special to get back. Items: Patch heals, Overclock speeds, Bulwark guards, Featherlight floats, Static Orb charges hits, Hot Cell explodes unless thrown.
